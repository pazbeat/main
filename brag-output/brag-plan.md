# /brag — imbir.kz (vertical, ~21 s)

**What it is:** online gift certificates for «Имбирь» — a network of Thai spa salons in Kazakhstan (9 salons, 7 cities).
**For whom:** anyone choosing a gift; the recipient gets Thai massage/spa with masters from Thailand.
**What sets it apart:** the whole gift is made on the phone in about two minutes; a beautiful PDF with a QR code goes to the recipient's email instantly or on a set date.
**Most impressive claim (site's own):** «Электронный сертификат за 2 минуты».
**Visual hook:** a gold stopwatch reading 2:00 that starts counting down.
**Real UI shown:** the mobile builder at imbir.kz/ru/create — design → program «Ты и Я» → name & greeting → delivery; then the email arrives.
**Tone:** polished, warm (brand plum + gold), confident.
**Share caption:** Подарить Таиланд — за 2 минуты. Открытка, программа, имя — и сертификат уже летит на email. imbir.kz

## Angle
"Таиланд за 2 минуты": a countdown runs while we build a real gift in the real builder; at 0:00 the certificate lands in the inbox and the salon atmosphere opens up.
