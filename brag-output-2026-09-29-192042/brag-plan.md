# /brag — «Имбирь»: Тепло (landscape, 22 s, own motion design, no stock footage)

**What it is:** «Имбирь» — Thai spa salons in Kazakhstan: 9 salons, 7 cities, masters from Thailand.
**Angle:** the brand promise is *warmth* («Тепло Таиланда»). In Kazakhstan that lands hardest against winter: frost outside, Thailand inside.
